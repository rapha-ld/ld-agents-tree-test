A new squad is joining and needs access. You are the admin who has to set that up
without giving them more permission than they need.

## Invite a new teammate
targets: account.members
accept: get-started.invite-members

## Add them to the squad's team
targets: account.team.members
accept: account.teams

## Check what that team is allowed to do
targets: account.team.permissions
accept: account.roles

## Find the SDK key they will need
targets: settings.environment.keys
accept: account.sdk-keys
